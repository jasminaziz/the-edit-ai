3 · The plan, in order

Structured as sittings (Jasmin's focused blocks) and sessions (Claude, code or Cowork). Sessions interleave between sittings; the sittings are the critical path. Estimated Jasmin time to merge: roughly 12–15 focused hours. The capture track runs parallel to the axis track and they only converge at the relaunch check.

Sitting 1 — Lock the axis and seed the data (~2.5 h)

  • A1. Ninety minutes with audit section 3: the seven fields, allowed values, Green/Amber/Red definitions, trustee-note format. Amend, then freeze. Everything downstream depends on it.
  • A2. Add headers jobs, data_location, trains_on_input, nonprofit_tier, dpia_flag, trustee_note, last_checked to G1:M1 of the tools tab. Two minutes, safe any time — main's fetcher ignores them.
  • Seed rows for B3. Fill the axis fields for two or three tools Jasmin knows cold, provisional fact values within the frozen allowed sets, her own first-pass dpia_flag and trustee_note. Leave one row deliberately empty so B3 builds the empty state. Fifteen minutes, corrected later by the A4 sourced pass.
  • Chip colours. Decide the three DPIA chip hex values (text-labelled chips, never colour alone; forest green and burnt orange need explicit sign-off to reuse). Either pre-decide or make the call live in the B3 session — the session cannot invent them.

Session A — B3, ToolCard and filters (code session)

The last big code block. Scope: render the axis fields on the card; DPIA flag as a text-labelled chip; nonprofit tier highlighted; trustee note inside the expanded verdict; CATEGORIES becomes the six comms jobs with contains-matching; three sector toggles above the grid (Has nonprofit pricing / Doesn't train on your content / DPIA green); last_checked display; place both C4 strings from the addendum verbatim.

Add to B3's scope, load-bearing: rows without completed axis fields are hidden from the grid, and the counter counts only visible rows. This is what makes Gate 1 self-resolving — the site relaunches smaller and grows back verified, exactly as F2 intends. Without it, triage alone doesn't make the counter true.
