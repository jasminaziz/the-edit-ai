## Per-row sourced notes

**Blotato.** US: privacy policy, data may be stored outside the EEA, no
residency or SCC commitment (blotato.com/privacy-policy). Training Unclear:
only training language is the Google-API compliance clause; no position on
user content. Vendor page shows an internal date discrepancy (header May
2026, footer Oct 2024). None: pricing page and search show no nonprofit
programme (blotato.com/pricing).

**Ideogram.** Unclear location: policy commits to nothing
(ideogram.ai/legal/privacy). Yes: "training the models that power our
Services" includes User Input, no opt-out described. None: nothing on
pricing page or anywhere vendor-published. Price changed since baseline:
Plus $20/mo ($15 annual) is now the floor (ideogram.ai/pricing).

**Granola.** US, AWS (docs.granola.ai privacy policy, effective 24 Jul
2026). Yes unless you opt out on Basic/Business: de-identified data used for
model improvement, opt-out in settings; Enterprise opted out by default;
third parties barred from training. Audio not retained once transcribed.
Change: the DPA is now published openly
(docs.granola.ai/help-center/policies/data-processing-addendum), no longer
on request. None: Startup and Student programmes exist, no nonprofit
(granola.ai/pricing).

**Submagic.** US servers, SCCs for ex-EEA transfers (submagic.co/privacy
sections 6.2-6.3). Training Unclear: notice silent on user uploads, only
YouTube-scoped improve language. None: nothing on pricing page or
vendor-published (submagic.co/pricing). Notice displays no last-updated
date despite referring to one.

**Gamma.** US: "your personal information will necessarily be accessed and
processed in the U.S.", SCCs (gamma.app/privacy). Yes unless you opt out on
all individual workspaces including Free; Team/Business excluded and locked
(help.gamma.app article 12281928). None: pricing page has no nonprofit
mention and the discount exists only as an open request on Gamma's feedback
board (ideas.gamma.app). Prices unverifiable server-side, same as 24 Aug.

**Gemini, consumer app.** Unclear: Privacy Hub makes no storage commitment
(support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961). Yes unless you opt out: trains
by default, Keep Activity is the opt-out, human-reviewed subset kept up to
three years, 72-hour retention even opted out. None on the consumer
product: only student offers (gemini.google/subscriptions). Tier renamed:
Google AI Plus $4.99/mo is a new cheaper entry, Pro $19.99/mo replaces
Advanced.

**Seedance via Dreamina.** Other: Bytedance Pte. Ltd. Singapore, data on US,
Singapore and Malaysia servers, UK/EEA supplemental terms (global Dreamina
privacy policy, 1 Apr 2025). A separate US-region policy (30 Apr 2026)
names TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC as operator. Yes: trains, no opt-out
described, both policy versions. None: nothing vendor-published.

**Grok, xAI.** US: "We process all of your personal information in the
United States" (x.ai Europe addendum, re-issued effective 24 Aug 2026, the
baseline date itself). UK representative Lionheart Squared Ltd. Yes unless
you opt out, and the two-opt-out structure is confirmed from primary
sources: xAI settings for Grok prompts, X settings for public X data, and
the second opt-out still leaves Grok learning from X-feature interactions
(x.ai/legal/faq; help.x.com about-grok). None: no nonprofit programme
anywhere vendor-published. Access model changed: Grok is free at grok.com
and in its apps, SuperGrok $30/mo; X Premium £8/mo bundles higher limits
but is not required, so the current cost cell's "Requires X Premium" is
wrong.
