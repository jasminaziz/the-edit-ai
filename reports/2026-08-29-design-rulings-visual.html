1. Hover: how does someone track the card they are on?

You are right that deleting the inversion removes the affordance. The affordance is not the problem. Inverting every colour inside the card is, because it forces every text colour to become an inline conditional, which is why ToolCard has 20 inline style blocks and no responsive classes. You can keep the signal and drop the mechanism.

Costs the rebuild
